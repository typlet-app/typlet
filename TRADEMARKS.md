# Trademark policy

The source code of Typlet is free software under the [GPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). The licence covers the code, **not the name "Typlet" or the Typlet logo**.

## You may

- say that your product works with, supports or is based on Typlet,
- use the name and logo to refer to the official project, for example in articles, tutorials and talks,
- distribute unmodified official builds under the Typlet name.

## You may not

- distribute modified versions or forks under the name "Typlet" or a confusingly similar name,
- use the logo for a fork or a different product,
- suggest that your product is official or endorsed by the Typlet project when it is not.

If you fork Typlet, please choose a new name and logo. That protects users from confusing official and modified builds, which matters for software that sees their keystrokes.
